# CRITICAL CAMPUSPATH REQUIREMENTS — UNIVERSAL COURSE ENGINE + ADVANCED AI VIVA

These requirements are MANDATORY.

They override any earlier implementation that assumes CampusPath is specifically for Artificial Intelligence, A*, BFS, DFS, programming, or any other particular subject.

==================================================
1. CAMPUSPATH MUST NOT BE HARD-CODED FOR AI COURSE
==================================================

CampusPath is a GENERAL-PURPOSE adaptive education platform.

Artificial Intelligence and A* are ONLY DEMO DATA.

They must NEVER define the application architecture.

Do NOT hard-code:

- A*
- BFS
- DFS
- Heuristics
- Minimax
- Artificial Intelligence terminology
- predefined AI course structures
- predefined AI prerequisite relationships
- AI-specific quiz templates

into the core learning system.

The system must dynamically learn the structure of whatever course/material the teacher provides.

Examples of courses CampusPath should be capable of supporting:
